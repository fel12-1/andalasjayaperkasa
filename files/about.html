OUR HISTORY

It started with a simple observation. Before AJP, our founder, Mr. Sugio, was involved in the scrap steel business around Medan. At the time, accessing oxygen for cutting steel was a significant challenge, and he saw an opportunity to provide a solution.

This led to the establishment of PT. Andalas Jaya Perkasa in 1991, initially supplying oxygen to meet the needs of local customers. Over the years, AJP continued to grow alongside the customers we serve. What began with oxygen expanded into a broader portfolio of argon, nitrogen, and liquefied gases, enabling us to support increasingly diverse applications and operational needs.
